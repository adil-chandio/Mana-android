## Full description
```
MAYA is a personal AI assistant built around one idea: your assistant should work for YOU — not for advertisers.

🎙️ TALK, DON'T TYPE
Wake word "Maya" se app khulta hai — bina touch ke. Tap-to-talk, offline voice commands (Bijli), and a smart brain that understands *why* you asked, not just *what*.

🧠 YOUR ASSISTANT, YOUR MEMORY
MAYA remembers what matters (facts, diary, routines) on YOUR device. Encrypted backup to your own private GitHub — keys never leave your phone.

🔒 PRIVACY BY DESIGN
• No ads. No trackers. No accounts.
• Microphone is on-device only — nothing is uploaded as audio.
• Password, OTP and card fields are never read, typed or logged.
• Every dangerous action asks first. "Maya ruk jao" cancels instantly.

📲 ALWAYS UP TO DATE
One-tap updates keep your settings and keys safe across every version.

🛠️ POWER TOOLS (in the Lab)
Wake word, voice guardian, screen reader automation with kill-switch, proactive smart suggestions, notification bridge, and a full self-diagnostic doctor.

Made with ❤️ + AI — 0 budget, 100% independent.
```
